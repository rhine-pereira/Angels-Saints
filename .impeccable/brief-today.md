# Surface: Today (+ app shell)

Scope: Android app shell (bottom nav Today / Library / Catechism) and the Today screen; Library, Catechism, content detail and name-day settings inherit the world. Mode: Read (Today, readings, content) / Operate (Library, Catechism search, name-day setup).

Audience/job: English- and Marathi-speaking Indian Catholics (Vasai–Mumbai) opening the app each morning for the day's feast and readings; occasional reference lookup.

Chosen direction: Kurus at the Village Gate (seed 152c6277, assigned index 6). Memorable moment: the season ring (year wheel) under a huge signpainted day numeral, today struck in marigold.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today is the painted plaque at the village kurus — date, feast, the Word — freshly garlanded each morning. Refuses the category default of burgundy/gold card stacks and stained-glass hero art.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk limewash ground (#F3F5F2), indigo ink and 1–2dp indigo trim rules (#22305C), marigold (#F2A007) only for what is live today, laterite rust (#8A3B24) for rubrics (Response/Acclamation), season vestment colour as a trim band. Yatra One signpainted display (Latin+Devanagari), Mukta UI, Martel reading text. Dark = lamp-lit indigo night (#121A33) with marigold glow. Flat fields, hairline trims, no gradients, no drop-shadow cards.

STORY: Visitor sees the date, season and feast at a glance, understands where today sits in the liturgical year, reads the readings, and (if it is their name-day) is greeted.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: huge Yatra One day numeral + weekday/month, season name and week in season colour trim. Centre: the year wheel ring (season arcs, marigold tick at today, countdown to next season inside). Below: indigo-ruled plaque with feast title and rank; readings plates I–IV begin beneath. Bottom nav (M3 NavigationBar) Today / Library / Catechism.

FORM: Wayside cross plaque (kurus) — rank 6 of grounded list; raises: fixed-anatomy reading plates (folio), ghosted neighbouring days (cathode), named seasonal trim pattern (cyclorama). Seed key 152c6277.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: drag the year wheel to scrub dates; plaque and readings follow; snaps to days with haptic tick; "Today" chip returns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
